# Internal review, 14 September 2026

This is the team's own review of the parts of MindEase that a licensed
clinician should also review before wider release: crisis handling, the
trauma-informed conversation rules, and the anti-dependency language. It is
not a clinical review. It records what was checked, against what, what was
changed, and what is still open. A clinician's review remains planned.

## 1. Crisis detection and response

Checked against: WHO *Preventing suicide: a resource for media professionals*
(safe-messaging principles), the Columbia Protocol's severity ladder (passive
wish to be dead → active ideation → method → intent/plan → preparatory acts),
and the Zero Suicide framework's emphasis on direct asking, means safety, and
warm hand-off.

| Item | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Tier ladder | The six tiers (none, distress, passive, active, plan, imminent) map to the Columbia ladder. | None |
| Direct asking | The prompt tells the model to ask plainly ("Are you thinking about ending your life?"). Safe: asking does not plant the idea. | None |
| Means safety | At plan/imminent the model asks whether the person can put distance between themselves and the means, and who can be with them in the next hour. | None |
| Preparatory acts | Giving things away, goodbye messages, "won't need them where I'm going", "won't be a problem much longer", "found a way out" were matched only by the model second opinion. | Added deterministic patterns; test cases added. |
| Phone keyboards | Curly apostrophes (’) defeated every pattern, so "I don’t want to be here" was missed. | All text normalised before matching; test added. |
| Arguing someone out of it | "You have so much to live for", "think of your family" are discouraged by safe-messaging guidance. | Reply guard removes them. |
| Language | No use of "commit suicide", "successful/failed attempt" anywhere in copy or prompts. | None |
| Helplines | KIRAN (1800-599-0019) was merged into Tele-MANAS and phased out in 2024. AASRA's listed number was the director's personal line. iCall's hours were wrong. | Corrected against official sources; verification date shown on the help page. |
| Hours | The card said lines were "available now" even when a limited-hours line was closed. | Each limited-hours line now shows "open now" or "closed now, opens 8am", and open lines sort first. |
| Model failure in a crisis | The fallback reply pointed at "the crisis lines on screen" without naming one. | Fallback now names Tele-MANAS 14416 and asks whether the person is safe. |
| Softer signals | Passive-tier phrases such as "I'm a burden" now ask before showing help, so a bad day is not treated like an emergency; explicit language still shows help at once. | Implemented; thresholds documented in `lib/crisis/surface.ts`. |

Open for a clinician: the strength thresholds for "ask first" versus "show at
once"; the 48-hour "three very low messages" trigger; whether the model should
ever ask about a plan (it does at active tier).

## 2. Trauma-informed conversation rules

Checked against: SAMHSA's six principles (safety; trustworthiness and
transparency; peer support; collaboration; empowerment, voice and choice;
cultural, historical and gender issues).

| Principle | Where it lives | Gap found | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Safety | Steady tone rule; no surprise reframes; crisis card | Model sometimes redirected to people in the same reply as a disclosure | A disclosure turn now gets a one-reply rule: acknowledge, believe, leave room; no redirect, no service, no number. |
| Trustworthiness | Mirror shows every read; memory asks before keeping | — | — |
| Peer support | Pointing to real people (§5) | — | — |
| Collaboration | "We can stay with this, or talk about something else. Your call." | — | — |
| Voice and choice | Corrections to the read; forget in one tap | Model invented past events ("remember the deadline last month") | Invented-history guard: any reference to the past must match a memory or the conversation. |
| Cultural and gender | Situation lines: caste-based violence (14566), women (181, 14490), child (1098), cyber (1930), LGBTQIA+, sexual violence | — | — |
| Never probe | Guard flags "why did you…" and "what happened" after a disclosure | — | — |
| Never label | Guard flags PTSD, dissociation, attachment style applied to the person | — | — |

Open for a clinician: the disclosure word list; whether "believe them" copy is
right for every disclosure type; teenage routing (currently gated off).

## 3. Anti-dependency language

Checked against: the product's own hard line (`docs/mindease-system-prompt.md`)
and the failure modes listed in the seven-advantages brief.

- The reply guard removes availability promises, secrecy, special-bond claims,
  claimed feelings, discouraging professional help, and invitations to keep
  talking here.
- The live language eval (`npm run eval:language`, 15 adversarial probes)
  passes 15/15 as sent.
- The model no longer adapts itself from ratings or sentiment; corrections are
  context, not calibration.

Open: a wider probe set in Tamil, Hindi and mixed-script messages.

## 4. Grounding frameworks

The five frameworks are used as ideas, never named; the guard removes framework
jargon and any attachment-style label even when the person asks for one.

## 5. Translations

Crisis, help-page, how-it-works and reliance copy in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu,
Kannada and Malayalam was reviewed by a second pass for meaning, register and
placeholders (see the session log). Wording flagged for a native speaker:
"anticipation", "disgust", "missed", and the crisis paragraph on not talking
anyone out of how they feel.

## 6. Hospitals shown in a crisis

`lib/safety/hospitals.ts` lists public mental-health institutions. Each row
carries the official URL it was checked against, and every phone number was
re-fetched from that page on 14 September 2026 before inclusion. Rows without a
confirmable official number carry no phone (the card then shows directions and
Tele-MANAS). Dropped for lack of an official source: Calcutta Pavlov Hospital,
Government Hospital for Mental Care Visakhapatnam (no official number), Modern
Psychiatric Hospital Tripura, IMHANS Kozhikode (not an emergency facility). The
Tele-MANAS state-cell list (telemanas.mohfw.gov.in/TMCCells, site updated 13 May
2026) is shown for every state as "who answers 14416 here", never as a place to
walk into. Live map results are added only when a Places key is configured.
KIRAN is not listed anywhere: it was merged into Tele-MANAS in 2024.

The translation review's proposed fixes (grammar, register, lost qualifiers in
Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam) were applied in the same pass.
